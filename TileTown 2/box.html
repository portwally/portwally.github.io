For the Apple IIgs

TileTown 2

An isometric city builder - your city now stands up from the map.

The sequel to TileTown. Lay the roads, run the power, zone homes, shops and factories - and watch them rise out of the ground. Skyscrapers stand tall, chimneys smoke, cars and buses face the way they drive, and trains run on the rails behind the buildings.

Written from scratch in 65816 assembly by Walter Tengler, for an Apple IIgs with 8 MB and an accelerator.

On the back of the box

Buildings stand up from the map.

TileTown 2 draws its city in 32 × 16 diamonds, so houses, offices and factories rise out of the ground - and cars, trains and tornadoes go behind them.

  1. Tall buildings stand up from the map
  2. Chimneys smoke over the factories
  3. A stadium - its score goes up
  4. The sea
  5. The toolbox: pick what to build; the price shows at the bottom
  6. The menus: City, Options, Disasters

RequiresAn Apple IIgs with 8 MB, an accelerator (it runs from 8 MHz and is at its best at 16 MHz) and a drive for an 800K 3.5" ProDOS disk. Or an emulator.

Inside the manual

Everything from TileTown, too

  • A living cityOn a 128 × 128 map. Homes, shops and factories grow in three sizes once they have power, a road and people who want them. Commuters drive to work and back.
  • 17 things to buildRoads, rail, power lines, homes, shops, industry, coal and nuclear power plants, police and fire stations, hospitals, schools, parks, a stadium, a seaport, an airport.
  • Over water, under waterBridges over water, level crossings, and rail tunnels under lakes and rivers.
  • Neighbourhoods that changeLand value, traffic, pollution and crime spread through the neighbourhoods, and people ask for what the city lacks.
  • Maps and graphsHeat maps of pollution, crime, land value, traffic, police, fire, health, schools, population and power. Graphs over 10 or 100 years.
  • A budget with slidersTaxes, roads, police and fire.
  • Five disastersFire, flood, tornado, earthquake and nuclear meltdown - or switch them off.
  • Advice and a scoreMilestones and monthly advice, a city evaluation with a score, and a query tool.
  • Easy to playMenus, pause and three speeds, a help screen, mouse and keyboard.
  • Eight save slotsEach city under its own name.
  • Sound and musicSound effects and eight relaxed tunes on the Ensoniq sound chip.
  • Free playNo goal, no deadline - just your city.

System requirements

  • An Apple IIgs with 8 MB of memory
  • A drive that boots an 800K 3.5" ProDOS disk
  • An accelerator - ZipGS, TransWarp GS or similar. It runs from 8 MHz and is at its best at 16 MHz.

or

  • An emulator such as MAME - on the Mac, Ample or EmuBuddy - set up as an Apple IIgs with 8 MB and the CPU type "16 MHz ZipGS"

A mouse is easier; the keyboard works too.
