Hit

Your streak grows, your next weapon is one stage up, and you earn points. In Beer mode, they owe you one.

Miss

You drop back to your base stage. In Beer mode, you owe them a beer.

Escape

Ran out of the blast on foot? You climb a stage and earn points for the dodge.

How it works

Launch. Warn. Run.

Share invite codes, pick your target and fire at their real position. They see it coming in real time on their phone.

01

Add friends

Share your 6-character invite code, or enter theirs. Only friends can see or target you.

02

Find them on radar

Your friends show up on a dark tactical map at their live GPS position.

03

Launch

Fire your current streak weapon at their real location — if they're in range.

04

They get the alert

An incoming-strike alarm with a countdown and how far they need to run.

05

Run or shield

Get out of the blast radius on foot, or burn points on a defense.

06

The server decides

At impact, the server checks where they are. Hit, escaped, or intercepted — no arguing.

Features

Friendly warfare, done right

Always a fair chance

Every strike gives the target enough warning to jog out of the blast radius — plus a head start to react.

Hot streak

Each hit makes your next shot one stage bigger. Chain hits to go from rocks to real-world missile specs.

Defenses

Smoke Screen, Iron Dome, a 30-minute Bunker, or a Decoy that sends missiles to the wrong place.

Live strike map

Follow your missile in flight or zoom out for the whole trajectory, with ETA and blast radius.

Beer ledger

Every hit and miss is tallied per friend. Mark beers as settled once they've been bought in real life.

Reload & ammo

Each weapon has its own reload time and daily ammo — big missiles are rare, so make them count.

Training Drone

Deploy a practice target 20 m away and try everything solo. No points or beers at stake.

Private by design

Only friends see your location, and you can go off-grid at any time. No ads, no tracking.

Stake modes

Play for beer or glory

Pick your stakes in Settings. Either way, the server keeps score — no arguing about who hit whom.

Beer mode Default

The classic. Every hit means they owe you a beer; every miss means you owe them.

  • Running tally per friend, in both directions
  • Settle Up once the beers are bought in real life
  • No real money ever changes hands in the app

Points mode

Just points, rank and bragging rights — no real-world stakes.

  • Climb from Recruit to Supreme Commander
  • Spend points on defenses
  • Perfect for friends who don't drink

Play safe

BeerTag is played outdoors in the real world. Stay aware of your surroundings, never play while driving or cycling, and never run into traffic. The beer is between you and your friends — BeerTag doesn't sell or deliver alcohol. Please drink responsibly, and only where you're of legal drinking age.
