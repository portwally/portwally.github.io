Tap. Flip. Chain.

Easy to pick up, surprisingly deep: the trick is choosing which way things fall.

1

Tap

Tap two or more touching gems of the same color to clear them. Every tap counts, so pick wisely.

2

Flip

Swipe, or tip your phone, and gravity changes. Every gem slides the new way and fresh groups line up.

3

Chain

Line up a big group with a flip and it clears by itself. A well-timed flip sets off a whole chain reaction.

Four worlds to flip

Switch any time, even mid-level. One theme is free, the others unlock as you collect stars. Each has its own art, sounds and particles.

One puzzle. Everyone. Every day.

The Daily Puzzle is the same board for every player, built from the date. Beat it, then share your result without spoiling the solution.

Little things that matter

💥

Rockets, bombs & combos

Clear 5 at once for a rocket, 7 for a bomb. Put two specials side by side and tap one for a combo.

⚓

Obstacles with attitude

Anchored gems, heavy stones that only fall one way, crates, sticky tiles and one-way walls.

🔷

Colorblind shapes

One switch gives every color its own shape: circle, square, triangle, star and diamond. Try it on the board above.

📱

Tilt to flip

Prefer motion? Tip your phone to flip gravity. It's off by default, so it never surprises you.

⚖️

Fair by design

A solver checks that every level can be beaten without boosters. You never have to pay to win.

🎵

Feels physical

Gems slide, overshoot and bounce. Every flip direction has its own sound and haptic pattern.

50 levels, five chapters

From your first tap to Gravity Masters. The first twelve levels each teach one idea, then the puzzles get properly twisty.

  • Earn up to three stars per level
  • Lives refill over time, and practice mode is always free
  • Coins for hammers, undos and extra flips
  • Stars unlock new themes

No ads. No tracking. No account.

Flipside doesn't collect anything about you. Your progress stays on your iPhone.
