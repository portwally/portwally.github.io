Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: Cuida24 keeps your health data on your device only. There is no server and no account. The app shows a small Google AdMob ad; your health data is never shared with the ad service. We, the developer, never receive or have access to any of your data.

1. Who we are

Cuida24 is an iPhone and iPad app developed by Walter Tengler, Portugal (see Legal Notice). This policy explains which data the app uses and how it is protected.

2. Data you enter in the app

To work, the app stores the data you enter or import yourself:

  • Profiles: name, date of birth (optional), sex (optional) and profile colour;
  • Lab results, with units, reference ranges, laboratory and date;
  • The original documents you import (PDF) and scanned or photographed pages;
  • Blood pressure and pulse readings, with date, arm, position, tags and notes;
  • Medical reports kept in the document archive and their text, for searching.

This is health data. It is stored only in your device's local storage, protected by iOS encryption. It is not sent to any server of ours or of third parties, and Cuida24 has no iCloud sync.

3. Camera, photos and text recognition

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a report or read your blood pressure monitor's display. Images are analysed on the device with Apple's text recognition. Images taken to read the display are not kept; scanned pages are stored as a PDF in the app, with the report.
  • Photos: the app only gets the photos you pick in the system picker, never your whole library.
  • Files: the PDFs and images you import are read on the device; a copy is stored in the app.

4. Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional and only active if you turn it on in Settings and allow it in iOS.

  • Writing: Cuida24 can save the blood pressure and pulse readings you record in Cuida24 to Apple Health.
  • Reading: Cuida24 can show blood pressure readings from other monitors stored in Apple Health. These readings are only read and displayed; they are not copied into Cuida24, not stored in iCloud and not shared with anyone.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or any purpose other than showing you your own values.

You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Cuida24.

5. Other permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID: the app lock uses iOS authentication. The app never has access to your biometric data.
  • Notifications: measurement reminders are local notifications created on the device.

6. Exports and backups

These are only created when you ask for them, and they go wherever you save or share them:

  • Backup: an encrypted file (AES-256) protected by a password you choose. Without that password nobody can open it, not even us.
  • PDF report and CSV files: not encrypted. When you share them (for example by email with your doctor), the rules of the service you choose apply.

If you use iPhone backups to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include Cuida24's data in those backups, encrypted by Apple, according to your device settings.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, Cuida24 shows an ad at the bottom of the screen through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google Ireland Limited.

  • What Google may collect: the ads SDK may collect technical device data such as IP address, device model and operating system, device identifiers and your interactions with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • No cross-app tracking: Cuida24 does not ask for tracking permission and gives no access to the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent (Google's form) before requesting ads. You can change your choice in Settings › Privacy › Ad choices.
  • Your health data is never shared: profiles, lab results, readings, documents and Apple Health data are never sent to Google or used to choose ads.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses data from partner apps.

8. What we don't do

  • We collect no usage data, statistics or crash reports from the app;
  • We use no cross-app tracking and no third-party SDKs other than Google AdMob;
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your health data to anyone;
  • We do not ask for registration, an account or an email address.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

As your data stays on your device only, we do not process it and cannot view, correct or delete it for you. You are fully in control:

  • correct or delete any value, report, reading or document in the app;
  • delete a profile and all its data in Settings;
  • export your data (CSV, PDF or backup);
  • delete the app to remove all data from the device.

You can always lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Portugal: CNPD).

10. Medical notice

Cuida24 is not a medical device and does not diagnose. It shows the values and reference ranges given by your laboratories and the limits published by the European Society of Cardiology (ESC). Always consult your doctor. Cuida24 is an independent app, not affiliated with the Portuguese National Health Service (SNS) or the SNS 24 portal.

11. Children

The app is not intended for children. Profiles for underage family members are created and managed by parents or guardians, and the data stays on their device.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.
