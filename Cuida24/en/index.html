Lab results

From the lab's PDF to a chart, in seconds

Import the PDF or scan the paper report with the camera. Cuida24 reads the values, units and reference ranges, and you confirm them before saving.

  • Every value highlighted in the original document
  • Blood count, glucose, cholesterol, kidney, liver, thyroid, urine and more
  • You can also enter values by hand
Trends

See how your values change over the years

Every test has its own chart, with the reference band given by each laboratory. Out-of-range values are highlighted, and the yearly trend is calculated for you.

  • Minimum, maximum, average and share within range
  • Comparison with the previous result in every report
Blood pressure

Frequently asked questions

Does Cuida24 connect to SNS 24?

No. Cuida24 is an independent app, not linked to the Portuguese National Health Service (SNS) or the SNS 24 portal. Import the PDFs you get from your lab or scan your paper reports.

Which laboratories are supported?

Cuida24 reads PDF and paper reports from Portuguese laboratories and hospitals. As formats vary, it always shows the values it read for you to confirm before saving, and remembers your corrections for the next report from the same lab.

Which blood pressure monitors work with the camera?

Monitors with a digital segment display and the SYS, DIA and PUL labels next to the numbers. Always check the values read; you can also enter them by hand.

Does my data go to the cloud?

No. It stays on your device only. You can create an encrypted backup and keep it wherever you like.

Does Cuida24 replace my doctor?

No. Cuida24 is not a medical device and does not diagnose. It helps you organise your data and take it to your appointment.
