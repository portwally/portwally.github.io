Privacy Policy

GS Remap does not collect anything.

The app. GS Remap works entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features. Pictures you load, convert and save stay in the folders you choose and are never sent anywhere.

Settings. The app remembers your choice of font on your Mac, in its own preferences. Nothing else is stored.

This website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no tracking. GitHub, as the host, may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs; see the GitHub privacy statement for details. The download link leads to GitHub.

Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Last updated: October 2026.
