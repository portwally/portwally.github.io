Briefing // 001

The galaxy is at war

An ancient alien threat has returned. Entire civilizations have vanished, and humanity’s last defense is a single experimental combat fighter.

Take control, master devastating weapons, and fight your way through the unknown: from a space station at dusk, past red fortresses, ice fields and a toxic marsh, through the void and into the core, where the last of them waits.

Combat systems

Built for the fight

Classic arcade shooting in the tradition of the 1980s, on a machine that was never meant to scroll this smoothly.

01

Advanced combat

Tap fire for single shots, or hold it to charge a blast that strikes five times as hard. Capsules drifting through every stage give you red, blue or yellow weapons.

02

The pod

An armoured pod docks on your nose or your tail and fires your weapon forward or backward. Send it ahead to hover as a shield, and call it back when you need it.

03

Ten alien worlds

From the station at dusk to the core: ten stages, each with its own sky, terrain, colours and weather, and walls you will not survive flying into.

04

Epic boss battles

Ten bosses, each fighting its own way: claws that strike out, homing orbs, bouncing shots, rams and charges. Find the weak point and hit it while it is open.
