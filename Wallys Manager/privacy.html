Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Wally's Manager is a macOS app that organizes your own software projects. It runs on your Mac and has no server of its own.

Data the app uses

  • Folders and files on your Mac that you point it to: your homepage folder, your source folders and Xcode projects, and Apple IIgs disk images. They are read, not uploaded.
  • The list of projects it builds is stored in a file in your Library folder, on your Mac only.
  • An optional App Store Connect API key and GitHub token are stored in your macOS Keychain.

Network requests the app makes

  • Apple's public App Store lookup (itunes.apple.com), with the App Store IDs of your apps, to read versions, dates, ratings and icons.
  • GitHub's API (api.github.com), with the names of your repositories, to read the latest releases.
  • App Store Connect (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com), only if you add your own API key, to read the review status of your apps.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no account and no tracking.

This website

This website (portwally.github.io) uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter that does not use cookies and does not track personal information. The skin you pick on this page is remembered in your browser only.

Children's privacy

Neither the app nor this website collects personal data, so they do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
