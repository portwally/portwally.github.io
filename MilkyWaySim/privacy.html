Milky Way Sim Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Milky Way Sim collects no data at all. Nothing about you or how you use the app ever leaves your Mac.

What the app collects

Nothing. Milky Way Sim:

  • does not collect, store or send any personal data;
  • does not track how you use it, and has no analytics;
  • has no advertising and no in-app purchases;
  • needs no account and no sign-in;
  • does not use the internet: it has no network access at all. All its star data and pictures are inside the app.

What stays on your Mac

The app keeps a few things for you, only on your own Mac, inside the app's own private folder: the flights you save, the log of places you have reached, and your settings (the cockpit's look, sound and music on or off, the music's volume, and whether you have seen the introduction). They are never sent anywhere, and nobody else can see them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, libraries for analytics or advertising, or trackers. The astronomical data in it comes from public scientific catalogues; using them sends nothing anywhere.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
